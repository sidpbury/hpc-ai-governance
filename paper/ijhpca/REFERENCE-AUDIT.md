## Removed from the journal bibliography

The journal version removes several references that were useful during broad literature exploration but were either tangential to the final contribution or unnecessary once stronger published references were available. This includes the Silicon Mirror anti-sycophancy paper, the general Slurm LLM deployment study, and several broad/tangential agent papers. The goal is a smaller bibliography whose purpose is clear to reviewers.
